# 风格：电影级写实仿真人短剧

## 中文

电影级写实、仿真人短剧质感；自然肤色与真实毛孔；纪实肩扛/过肩/缓推运镜；光影服务情绪，忌舞台灯与炫光。禁止：动漫、二次元、科幻全息沙盘、虚幻引擎炫技光效、卡通比例。

## English

Cinematic photoreal short-drama look; natural skin with visible pores; documentary handheld / OTS / slow push-in; lighting serves emotion, no stage spots or neon glows. Forbidden: anime, cartoon, sci-fi hologram sandbox, Unreal Engine spectacle lighting, exaggerated cartoon proportions.
